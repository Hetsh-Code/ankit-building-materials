Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding site delivery, wholesale terms, billing, and order placement.

What is the delivery radius along Radhanpur-Kandla Highway? ▼
We provide express site delivery covering Santalpur, Radhanpur, Varahi, Adesar, Samakhiali, Patan city, Harij, Sami, and extending towards Gandhidham and Tharad. Heavy orders (cement, steel, pipes) are delivered via tractor trolleys or 10-wheeler trucks directly to your construction coordinates.
Do you provide GST invoices and Manufacturer Test Certificates (MTC)? ▼
Yes, 100%. We provide valid GST input tax invoices for all purchases, along with original mill test certificates (MTC) for TMT rebar steel, cement batch certificates, and ISI test compliance reports for PPE safety gear.
Can I place bulk orders directly through WhatsApp or phone? ▼
Absolutely! Simply call us at +91 97148 58660 or use our website's Quote Cart to send your list via WhatsApp. Our team responds with quotation breakdown, dispatch vehicle details, and payment options within minutes.
What are the working hours of the Santalpur yard? ▼
We are open 7 days a week from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM to support morning construction pours and late-evening highway project emergencies.
Do you supply industrial safety items (PPE) compliant with audit standards? ▼
Yes. As shown on our highway billboard, "Your Safety Our Priority" is our core pillar. We stock ISI marked industrial helmets, high-visibility EN471 Class 2 reflective jackets, steel-toe boots (200J impact rated), fall arrest harnesses, and protective eye gear.
